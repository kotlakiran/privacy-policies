# LittleLeap Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LittleLeap handles information in the app.

## Information stored on the device

LittleLeap stores child profiles, settings, lesson progress, stars, streaks, and collectibles locally on the device so the app can provide lessons and remember progress. This local learning data is not sold or used for advertising.

## Optional parent sign-in and Firebase services

The app does not require a child to create an account. A parent may optionally use Google Sign-In on Android. If a parent chooses to sign in, Google Firebase Authentication processes the Google account identifier and authentication credential needed to authenticate that parent.

Authenticated Android builds may read encrypted content keys from Firebase Cloud Firestore. Firebase App Check helps protect those requests. LittleLeap does not use Firebase to sell personal information or to serve advertising.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## Information we do not intentionally collect

LittleLeap does not use advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. It does not use the Advertising ID, microphone, camera, location, chat, or social features. The app does not intentionally collect a child's name, email address, contact list, precise location, photos, voice recordings, or health information.

## Children's privacy

LittleLeap is designed for children and families. The app does not require a child to provide personal information. Optional account actions are intended for a parent or guardian, and parents should supervise use of the app and manage any Google account sign-in.

## Data retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on the device until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. A parent can stop using Google Sign-In through the app and can request help with account-related data by contacting us.
