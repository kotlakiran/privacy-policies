Delete your Laser Grid account and data

Developer: Kiran Kotla Studios

Signing in to Laser Grid is optional. Local play does not need an account. If you created an online account, you can ask us to delete it and the cloud data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a message to kiran.kotla90@gmail.com with the subject "Laser Grid account deletion".
  2. Send it from the address you used to sign in, or include your in-game display name or player ID so we can find the account.
  3. We will confirm the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in record (Firebase Authentication account).
  • Your online profile, cloud progress, scores and leaderboard entries.
  • Friend, invitation and online match records linked to your account.
  • Notification registration tokens stored with your profile.

What may be kept

Aggregated analytics that cannot identify you may be kept. Limited records may be kept for up to 90 days where needed for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations, and are then deleted.

Deleting data without deleting your account

You can ask us to delete only your cloud progress, profile or match history by sending the same message and saying which data to remove. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
