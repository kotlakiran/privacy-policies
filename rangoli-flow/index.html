Rangoli Flow Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Rangoli Flow respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Rangoli Flow mobile application.

Information Collection and Use

Rangoli Flow does not collect, sell, or share personally identifiable information.

Game progress, local scores, unlocks, and settings are stored on your device using the app's local database. The current Android app does not require an account or network permission.

Permissions

VIBRATE: Used to provide optional haptic feedback during gameplay.

Third-party services

The current Android app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, sign-in, payments, or third-party data sharing.
