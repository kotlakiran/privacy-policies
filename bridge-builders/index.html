Bridge Builders Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Bridge Builders stores game progress and settings locally on your device using Android Room and DataStore. The game can be played offline.

Optional online features

If Firebase is configured for a distribution build and you choose to use cloud resume, Firebase Authentication may create an anonymous player identifier and Firestore may store a serialized saved match. Online features are not required for local play.

Permissions and advertising

The app does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, or phone permissions. Vibration is used for haptic feedback. The current release contains no advertising SDK or in-game advertising.

Account and data deletion

To request deletion of an account and its associated cloud game data, email kiran.kotla90@gmail.com from the account email address and identify Bridge Builders. We will delete account-associated authentication records and cloud saved-match data under our control. Aggregated, non-identifying analytics may be retained. Local data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, email kiran.kotla90@gmail.com.
