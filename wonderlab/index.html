WonderLab — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

WonderLab ("the app") is a 3D science learning app for children, developed by Kiran Kotla. This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to all users, including children.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no in-app purchases, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The app does not request any Android runtime permissions and does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Learning progress (stars and completed experiments) and settings (such as subtitles) are saved only on your device. This data never leaves the device, is not backed up by the app, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Voice

The teacher's voice is produced by your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The app sends text to that engine only; it does not record or listen to audio and does not use the microphone or camera.

Children

The app is designed for children. Because no personal information is collected from anyone, no personal information is collected from children. Real-world experiments shown in the app include safety notes and should only be done with a grown-up.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
