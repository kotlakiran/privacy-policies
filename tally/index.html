Tally — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Tally is an offline-first habit, meal, reminder and notes app published by Kiran Kotla Studios. This policy explains what information Tally handles and when it leaves your device.

Information stored on your device

Habits, counts, meals, reminders, notes, settings, voice-note records and progress are stored locally on your device. Tally does not operate its own account database or sell personal information.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you explicitly enable backup, Tally uploads an app-data backup to the private appDataFolder in your own Google Drive. The backup is associated with your Google account and is not accessible to Tally or other apps. You can disable or delete it at any time.

Optional Health Connect access

If you grant permission, Tally reads steps, active energy, sleep sessions and hydration from Health Connect. It does not write or modify Health Connect records. Health data is used for in-app context and coaching suggestions. Health data is not used for advertising or sold.

Health data leaves the device only when you actively ask the AI coach for a response. A short summary is sent to Google's Gemini service through Firebase AI Logic for that response and is not retained by Tally as a separate health-data history.

AI coach and voice features

When you use the AI coach, your question and limited app context may be sent to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic to generate the response. When you start voice capture, microphone audio is processed for the requested voice action and voice records remain on the device unless you explicitly export or back them up.

Notifications and permissions

Notifications are used only for reminders and summaries that you schedule. Vibration is used for haptic feedback. Microphone, Drive and Health Connect access are optional and require your permission. Tally does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Deletion and retention

You can delete habits and other app data in Tally, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Delete any optional Google Drive backup from Google Drive. Revoking permissions stops future access.

Children

Tally is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
