Privacy Policy for Rescue Fireflies: Grid Puzzle

Developer: Kiran Kotla Studios
App: Rescue Fireflies: Grid Puzzle (Android package com.kirankotla.rescuefireflies.game)
Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Rescue Fireflies app (the "App") handles, why, and the choices you have. The App does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or files.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (current level, stars, hints left, daily challenge results) and settings such as sound on/off are saved only on your device in the App's private storage. We do not receive or upload this information. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information from the App.

3. Internet use

  • More Games list: When you open "More Games", the App downloads a public list of our other games and their icons from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). This request does not include any personal information, but, like any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Play links: Tapping a game in the list opens its page in the Google Play Store app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) so that players can optionally watch a rewarded video to earn an extra hint. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. When ads are enabled, the App asks Google to treat requests as child-directed and to show only G-rated content.

When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process information such as your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and the device advertising ID, to show, measure and protect against fraud in ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load the More Games list and optional rewarded ads.
  • Advertising ID permissions (added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK): used only by Google for advertising as described above.

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

6. Children

The App is a family-friendly puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are marked as child-directed so that Google limits data use under its policies for children.

7. Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Because we do not receive personal information, we do not retain any on our servers.

8. Your choices and data deletion

You can delete all App data at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App. You can choose not to watch rewarded ads.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Kiran Kotla Studios at kiran.kotla90@gmail.com.
