Privacy Policy for Rescue Fireflies

Effective Date: October 3, 2024

Rescue Fireflies ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you use the Rescue Fireflies mobile application (the "App").

1. Information Collection and Use

  • No Personal Data Collection: Rescue Fireflies does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device IDs.
  • Local Preference Data: Game progress, unlocked levels, star ratings, high scores, and audio settings are saved locally on your device using Android DataStore. This data never leaves your device.

2. Permissions

  • VIBRATE / HAPTICS (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide optional haptic feedback when interacting with game elements. You can disable haptics in the in-game Settings menu at any time.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

  • The App operates 100% offline.
  • The App does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services.

4. Children's Privacy

  • Rescue Fireflies is suitable for players of all ages, including children.
  • Because we do not collect any personal data, we fully comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR guidelines.
