Privacy Policy for Rescue Fireflies: Grid Puzzle

Developer: Kiran Kotla Studios
App: Rescue Fireflies: Grid Puzzle (Android package com.kirankotla.rescuefireflies.game)
Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Rescue Fireflies app (the "App") handles, why, and the choices you have. The App does not require an account, has no sign-in, and does not ask you to enter any personal details.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (current level, stars, hints left, daily challenge results) and settings such as sound on/off are saved only on your device in the App's private storage. We do not receive or upload this information. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Information we collect

The Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) automatically collects and shares certain information when the App requests or displays ads. This can include the device IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), ad and app interactions such as launches, taps, and video views, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Advertising ID and App Set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The data is encrypted in transit. We do not ask you to provide an email address or create an account.

3. Internet use

  • More Games list: When you open "More Games", the App downloads a public list of our other games and their icons from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). This request does not include any personal information, but, like any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Advertising: Google Mobile Ads processes the information described above when ads are requested or shown. See Google's Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Play links: Tapping a game in the list opens its page in the Google Play Store app.

4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to offer a rewarded video for an extra hint. When the ads SDK is initialized and ads are requested or shown, it may collect and share IP address, ad and app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Learn more in Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure. The App configures ad requests as child-directed and limits ad content to a general-audience rating.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load the More Games list.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and related measurement. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

6. Children

The App is a family-friendly puzzle game. Ad requests are configured as child-directed and limited to a general-audience content rating. Google Mobile Ads may still process the device and ad-related information described above to serve and measure ads and help prevent fraud.

7. Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Advertising data is handled by Google; Google encrypts Mobile Ads SDK data in transit and describes its processing and retention in its Privacy Policy.

8. Your choices and data deletion

You can delete local game data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and manage ad personalization in Android settings. These device settings do not delete local game progress.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
