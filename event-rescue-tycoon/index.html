Privacy Policy for Event Rescue Tycoon

Developer: Kiran Kotla Studios
App: Event Rescue Tycoon (Android package com.kotlakiran.eventrescuetycoon)
Last updated: 18 November 2026

Event Rescue Tycoon is a single-player event-management game. It has no accounts or sign-in, and we, the developer, do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

Fictional gameplay, not real-world medical care

Version 0.1.1 and later contain fictional wedding, party and concert preparation: cleaning, repairing, sorting, decorating, finding lost event items and managing guest queues. The ambulance, patient-delivery and first-aid quiz mode has been removed. The game does not provide health features, first-aid instruction, medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

The game is not a real emergency-response service, medical device or substitute for professional medical care. If you have an older version with rescue-themed questions, do not use those questions or simplified actions as medical advice, certified first-aid training or instructions for a real emergency. For an actual emergency, contact your local emergency services and follow guidance from qualified professionals.

The game does not ask for or record your real symptoms, injuries, medical history or health measurements. Event scores describe fictional venue preparation, not the player's health.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, crew, upgrades, purchases you own and settings (sound, vibration, graphics, text size) are saved only on your device in the game's private storage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded videos (for example, double coins or a hint) using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). A video only plays when you choose to watch one. Ads are limited to content rated G.

When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostic data and your device advertising ID to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment details; we never see your card or payment information. The game only receives a purchase record (product and status) from Google Play so it can unlock or restore what you bought.

Permissions

Internet and network state: to load optional rewarded ads and check purchases. Advertising ID: used only by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above. The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security, retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. We do not run our own servers for this game and do not hold any of your personal information, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google for ads or purchases is governed by Google's privacy policy and settings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
