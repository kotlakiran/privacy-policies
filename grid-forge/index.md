# Grid Forge Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026

Grid Forge is a number puzzle game published by Kiran Kotla. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## Information we collect

Grid Forge does not require an account and does not collect personal information, payment information, health information, contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers.

## Network access

The app may connect to a GitHub-hosted catalog to display optional links for other games. This request does not include personal information. The catalog and its images are downloaded over HTTPS.

## Local storage

Game progress, preferences, rewards, and audio settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive or have access to this information.

## Children

Grid Forge does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. Questions can be sent to [kiran.kotla90@gmail.com](mailto:kiran.kotla90@gmail.com).
