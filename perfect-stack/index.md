# Perfect Stack Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 21, 2026

Perfect Stack is a 3D physics puzzle game published by Kiran Kotla.

## Information we collect

Perfect Stack does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. The current version does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or an online service.

## Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, settings, coins, unlocked themes, and level results locally on your device so the game can resume where you left off. This information is not sent to us or to third parties.

## Payments and advertisements

The current test build does not connect to Google Play Billing and does not serve advertisements. The shop and reward screens are local gameplay demonstrations and do not process payments or collect advertising data.

## Children's privacy

Because the game does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children or adults.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app's data practices change. Questions can be sent to [kiran.kotla90@gmail.com](mailto:kiran.kotla90@gmail.com).
