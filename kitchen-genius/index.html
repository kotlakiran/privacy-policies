Kitchen Genius Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Kiran Kotla Studios develops Kitchen Genius (com.kotlakiran.kitchengenius), an offline cooking and restaurant-management game.

Information collected and shared

Kitchen Genius does not collect or transmit personal information to us or third parties. This release has no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs, cloud saves, in-app purchases or social features.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, in-game coins, upgrades, restaurant statistics, puzzle results and preferences in the app's private storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. Android automatic backup is disabled for the app.

Voice guidance and device features

Optional spoken guidance uses Android's text-to-speech service to read game instructions. Kitchen Genius does not record speech or access your microphone. The behaviour of your chosen system text-to-speech provider is governed by that provider's settings and privacy policy. You can turn voice guidance off in the game. Optional vibration uses the device's haptic feedback.

Google Play

Google Play independently processes information when you download, update, rate or review an app, under Google's privacy policy. Kitchen Genius does not receive your Google account details.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you voluntarily include. We use it to respond to your request and do not sell it. Do not send sensitive information. You may request deletion of a support email by contacting us.

Deleting local data

You can remove game progress and preferences by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. There is no online account to delete.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players.

Changes

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change, and revise the effective date above.
