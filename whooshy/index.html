Whooshy — Privacy Policy

Whooshy – Slide the Arrows

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Short version: Whooshy does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads tracking, and no analytics. All game progress stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Whooshy (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a puzzle game developed by Kiran Kotla. This policy explains how the app handles information. If you have any questions, contact us at kiran.kotla90@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or any account sign-in.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves small amounts of game data locally on your device only, such as your current level, coins, unlocked styles, and sound/vibration settings. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

4. Children’s privacy

Whooshy is designed to be family-friendly and is suitable for children. Because the app collects no personal information, it complies with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation for children (GDPR-K). We do not knowingly collect any data from children or anyone else.

5. Permissions

The app requests only the VIBRATE permission, used to provide gentle haptic feedback during play. This permission does not access any personal information.

6. Advertising & analytics

The app contains no third-party advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. No data is sent to any third party.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any.

8. Internet access

The app is designed to work fully offline and does not transmit your information over the internet.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds a new feature), we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email kiran.kotla90@gmail.com.
